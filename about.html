Who YT Labels is

The work is printed packaging-label supply for B2B brands and their packaging teams. Category pages cover cosmetic and skincare labels for bottles, jars, tubes and multi-SKU sets; personal care and wash-care labels for shampoo, body wash, hand soap, laundry and home-care packs; and candle and fragrance labels for jars, lids, tins and gift boxes.

Supply can be on rolls or sheets, with a digital proof before sampling or bulk production. Constructions discussed on the site include clear PET, waterproof BOPP, vinyl, textured paper, matte or gloss laminate, and foil details where the artwork and quantity support them. A material name on a product card is a starting point. Adhesion, appearance and handling are reviewed against the actual container.

Formulation, filling and approval of product claims remain with the brand and its manufacturing partners. YT Labels does not publish a factory street address on this website. Do not treat a packaging photograph as a map of a plant or as a certification.

Who the buyers are

Typical buyers are packaging, purchasing and brand teams ordering labels for products they already make or private-label:

  • Skincare and cosmetic brands planning serum, lotion, toner, cream, oil and set labels.
  • Personal care and wash-care brands planning shampoo, body wash, hand soap, laundry and home-care labels.
  • Candle and home-fragrance brands planning jar, lid, tin and gift-box labels, including multi-scent ranges.
  • Operations teams who need roll specifications for a labeling machine, or sheets for hand application and short runs.

The product catalog is a reference for those conversations. Prices, minimums and lead times on a card are not a standing offer. Each construction is quoted on its own.

Low-MOQ projects can be reviewed for many cosmetic, wash-care and candle labels. There is no single published minimum or fixed price for every construction. A requested deadline stays a planning input until it is confirmed in the proposal.

Digital proof and physical sample

A digital proof can be prepared for review before production. The FAQ and product pages describe that proof as free. Use it to check text, layout, dimensions and artwork placement.

A digital proof does not confirm adhesion, wet-use durability, exact physical colour or machine application. When those properties matter, review a physical sample on the filled container. Samples can be arranged after artwork, material, size and proof details are confirmed. Sample cost and timing depend on material, finish and order requirements.

Bulk production starts after final artwork, material and order details are approved. Approving a screen proof is not approval of adhesive performance. The boundary is set out in the sample policy and the artwork proof guide.

How to read the buying guides

YT Labels publishes the guides on this website, including pages bylined YT Labels Editorial Team. A guide can explain a decision and list missing specifications. It cannot establish that an adhesive works on your bottle, that a file is print-ready, or that a retailer accepts your barcode.

Where a guide cites a technical document, follow that source and its stated scope. A data sheet for one construction does not describe another. Images captioned as AI-generated packaging examples are illustrations. They are not evidence of a customer order, a factory process, a certification or a measured test result.

This page does not add certification marks or a postal address. Confirm the specification that applies to your order in writing. Corrections to a guide go through the editorial policy.

Before you approve: separate layout approval from material approval. Keep the accepted file, the quoted construction and the sample scope attached to the same project. Read how the guides should be used.
