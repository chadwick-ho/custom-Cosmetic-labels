FAQ

Wash care label FAQ

Which materials work for shampoo and body wash bottles?

Waterproof BOPP, PET, vinyl and laminated options are common choices for wet-room personal care packaging.

Can you make labels for pump bottles?

Yes. We can support labels for pump bottles, tubes, spray bottles, jars and other personal care packaging formats.

Can the labels be supplied on rolls?

Yes. Roll labels can be prepared for manual application, small production or packaging operations.

Can I use the same style across multiple products?

Yes. We can help keep a consistent label structure across shampoo, conditioner, body wash, lotion and related product lines.
