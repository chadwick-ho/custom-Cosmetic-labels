FAQ

Wash care label FAQ

Which materials work for wash care and home care bottles?

Waterproof BOPP, PET, vinyl and laminated options are common choices for bathroom, laundry and repeated-use packaging.

Can you make labels for pump, spray and detergent bottles?

Yes. We can support labels for pump bottles, spray bottles, tubes, jars, detergent bottles and other wash care packaging formats.

Can the labels be supplied on rolls?

Yes. Roll labels can be prepared for manual application, small production or packaging operations.

Can I use the same style across multiple products?

Yes. We can help keep a consistent label structure across shampoo, conditioner, body wash, lotion, laundry and home care product lines.
