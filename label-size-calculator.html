How the calculation works

Label width = π × bottle diameter − seam gap. Label height is the usable straight-side height you enter. Choose the gap for your project; the calculator does not assume one is suitable for every container. It does not add print bleed or adhesive overlap.

Before you print

Cut a paper mockup and wrap it around the filled container. Check the shoulder, base curve, seam and front alignment. Ask the printer to confirm the final dieline, bleed and machine requirements.
