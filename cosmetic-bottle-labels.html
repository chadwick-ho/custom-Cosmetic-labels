Material performance

Choose a label finish that matches your cosmetic brand look

Cosmetic packaging often needs a clean brand impression, accurate color, smooth edges and material that stays neat on glass, plastic, amber, frosted or jar surfaces. Matte, glossy, clear, foil and custom shape options are available.

FAQ

Custom cosmetic label FAQ

Can you make custom sizes and shapes?

Yes. We can manufacture rectangle, round, oval, wraparound and die cut labels based on your artwork and bottle dimensions.

Are waterproof and oil-resistant cosmetic labels available?

Yes. Waterproof BOPP, PET, vinyl and laminated materials are available for cosmetic bottle and jar applications.

Can you help if I only have a logo and idea?

Yes. Send your logo, bottle photo and reference style. Our team can help prepare a digital proof for review.

Can I make small batch cosmetic labels first?

Yes. Low MOQ is available for many cosmetic label projects, and bulk production can follow after confirmation.
