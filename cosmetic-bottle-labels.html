FAQ

Custom Cosmetic Bottle Labels for Skincare and Beauty Packaging FAQ

Answers for B2B buyers preparing custom bottle label orders.

Can you make waterproof cosmetic bottle labels?

Yes. Waterproof BOPP, PET and vinyl are commonly used for cosmetic bottle labels that need moisture resistance.

Can you make oil-resistant labels for skincare packaging?

Yes. We can recommend laminated or oil-resistant material options based on bottle surface and product use.

Can I order a small batch for a new beauty brand?

Yes. Low MOQ is available for many cosmetic label projects, depending on material, size and finish.

Can you make clear labels for serum bottles?

Yes. Clear label material is a popular choice for glass serum bottles, amber bottles and clean skincare packaging.
