Best Waterproof Materials for Cosmetic and Supplement Bottle Labels

This guide helps B2B packaging buyers prepare clearer custom bottle label requirements before requesting a quote, digital proof or sample production.

Waterproof BOPP

Waterproof BOPP is a common option for bottle labels that need moisture resistance and clean roll label production.

PET and clear label material

PET and clear materials are useful when brands want a clean surface, transparent effect or durable label structure.

Vinyl and laminated labels

Vinyl and laminated options can support curved surfaces, custom shapes and stronger handling requirements.

Match material to bottle use

Cosmetic bottles may need oil-resistant options. Supplement bottles often need clear printing and stable roll label performance.

What to send for the next step

Prepare bottle type, label width, label height, order quantity, material preference, finish preference, artwork file and shipping country.
