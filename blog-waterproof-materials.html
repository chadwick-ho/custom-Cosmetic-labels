Best Waterproof Materials for Cosmetic and Wash Care Labels

Updated September 5, 2026 by YT Labels factory support team

Choose label materials based on bottle surface, wet-room use, product contact, finish preference and production quantity.

Waterproof BOPP

BOPP is a practical material for many cosmetic, shampoo, body wash and hand soap labels. It works well with laminated finishes and roll production.

Clear PET

Clear PET creates a clean transparent look for glass, amber, frosted and selected plastic bottles. It is often used for skincare and premium cosmetic packaging.

White vinyl

Vinyl can be useful when durability and flexible application are important. It is suitable for many personal care bottle surfaces.

Matte, gloss and foil finishes

Matte finishes feel soft and refined. Gloss finishes create brighter color. Foil accents help premium beauty packaging stand out.
