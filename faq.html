Custom Bottle Label FAQ

Answers to common questions about custom cosmetic bottle labels, supplement bottle labels, artwork, proofing, MOQ and production.

What is your MOQ?

MOQ depends on label size, material, finish and production method. Low MOQ is available for many custom bottle label projects.

Can I order a small batch first?

Yes. Small batch orders are available for many cosmetic and supplement bottle label projects.

Do you provide free digital proof?

Yes. We can prepare a free digital proof based on artwork, bottle size and label requirements before production.

Can you help if I do not have artwork?

Yes. Send your logo, text content, bottle size or reference image and our team can help prepare a proof.

Can I upload my logo or design file?

Yes. You can upload logo, label artwork, dieline, reference image or packaging design files through the inquiry form.

What file formats do you accept?

We prefer AI, PDF, PSD, EPS, SVG and high-resolution PNG files. A reference image can also be sent first.

How do I choose the right label size?

Measure bottle diameter, bottle height, available label area, label width and label height. A bottle photo also helps.

What materials do you recommend for cosmetic bottles?

Waterproof BOPP, clear PET, white vinyl and oil-resistant laminated materials are common choices.

What materials do you recommend for supplement bottles?

Waterproof BOPP, PET, white vinyl, matte paper and laminated materials are common choices.

Are your labels waterproof?

Waterproof material options are available, including BOPP, PET and vinyl.

Are the labels oil-resistant?

Oil-resistant options are available for cosmetic and skincare packaging.

Do you support roll labels?

Yes. Roll labels are available for production lines, packaging operations and higher quantity orders.

Can you make clear labels?

Yes. Clear labels are available for glass bottles, plastic bottles, amber bottles and selected packaging styles.

Can you make custom shapes?

Yes. Die cut labels can be made in custom shapes based on artwork and packaging requirements.

How long does sampling take?

Sampling time depends on material, finish and production schedule. We provide an estimated timeline after confirmation.

How long does bulk production take?

Bulk production time depends on quantity, material and finishing process.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes. Worldwide shipping is available. Delivery time depends on destination, order quantity and package weight.

How do I get a quote?

Send product type, bottle type, label size, quantity, material preference, finish preference, artwork file and shipping country.

Still have questions about your bottle label project?

Send bottle type, size, quantity, material preference and artwork. Our team will help create a free digital proof and provide a factory-direct quote.
