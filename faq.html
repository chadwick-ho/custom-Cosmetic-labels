Custom Label Manufacturing FAQ

Answers about artwork, free digital proof, materials, MOQ, sampling, production and shipping for cosmetic and wash care labels.

Ordering

What information do you need for a quote?

Please send product type, bottle photo, label width and height, quantity, material or finish preference, artwork status and shipping country.

Do you offer low MOQ?

Yes. Low MOQ is available for many custom cosmetic, wash care and candle label projects. MOQ depends on size, material and finish.

Can I order samples before bulk production?

Yes. Sampling can be arranged after artwork, material, size and proof details are confirmed.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes. Finished label orders can be shipped worldwide. Delivery time depends on destination, order size and shipping method.

Artwork and proof

Do you provide free digital proof?

Yes. We can prepare a digital proof or mockup for review before production.

What artwork files do you accept?

AI, PDF, PSD, EPS, SVG and high-resolution PNG files are preferred. Reference images can also be sent first.

Can you help if I do not have final artwork?

Yes. Send your logo, bottle details, text and preferred style. Our team can help prepare a proof for review.

Materials and production

Are waterproof materials available?

Yes. Waterproof BOPP, PET, vinyl and laminated materials are available for cosmetic and wash care bottle labels.

Can you make clear labels?

Yes. Clear PET labels are available for transparent bottles, frosted bottles, amber bottles and selected cosmetic packaging styles.

Do you support roll labels?

Yes. Roll labels are available for manual application, small batch production and larger packaging operations.

Can you make custom shapes?

Yes. Die cut labels can be produced based on your artwork and packaging requirements.

Candle and fragrance labels

What labels work for candle jars?

Jar labels, lid labels, box labels, clear PET, waterproof BOPP, textured paper and foil details are available based on the package and finish.

Can you make matching candle jar and gift box labels?

Yes. Matching jar, lid and gift box label sets can be prepared from the same artwork and scent variant system.

Do candle labels support small batch production?

Yes. Small batch candle and home fragrance label projects can be reviewed for roll or sheet production before a larger order.

Still preparing your label project?

Send the details you already have. We can help confirm the missing information before quote and proof.
