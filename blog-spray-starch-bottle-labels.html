Spray Starch Bottle Labels: Read the Front, Clear the Trigger

Published October 4, 2026 by YT Labels Editorial Team

At a laundry shelf, a spray starch bottle can be mistaken for almost any other trigger product if the front relies on colour alone. I would put the product name where a hand holding the neck does not cover it. Find the straight wall beneath the shoulder

Fit the trigger head and measure from the bottle shoulder to the base radius. Avoid taking the artwork around the compound curve just because the render looks smooth. Film label portfolios include conformable options for shaped bottles, but Avery Dennison's conformable-film overview is a selection starting point, not proof for this exact bottle.

The pictured label sits on the straight body and leaves the trigger free. If the bottle has a waist, finger groove or ribbed grip, include it in the dieline sketch. Ask the applicator which panel is presented during labelling.

Give the category name priority

“Spray Starch” should be easy to distinguish from a fabric refresher or stain remover. A shirt-and-fold icon supports that distinction; it should not replace the words. The approved directions, cautions and other market copy belong on a properly sized back area supplied by the brand's responsible team.

I would line up the final packs next to the fabric refresher and stain remover variants. If the names disappear at arm's length, change the hierarchy before changing the ink finish.

Proof after filling and normal handling

Trigger bottles may be gripped, sprayed and set near a damp ironing area. UPM Raflatac's household-label overview treats contents and end-use exposure as material-selection factors. It does not establish resistance for this untested illustration. Request an applied sample on the production bottle and check print readability and edges under your planned use.

Do not infer a chemical-resistance rating from a word such as waterproof. Specify the bottle resin, product contact expectations and cleaning process to the label supplier, then agree on the actual test method and acceptance criteria.

Brief the label line as well as the designer

Send bottle and trigger samples, label dimensions, approved front and back artwork, number of versions, quantities and application method. For automatic application, list unwind direction, core, gap and maximum roll diameter.

A digital proof catches a wrong name; an applied sample catches a trigger collision or a drifting front panel. I would require both before authorising the full roll order.
