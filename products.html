How to select a label type

Share the bottle photo and use condition if you are unsure. We can recommend a material and finish before proofing.

Cosmetic packaging

Usually prioritizes clean brand presentation, premium finish and precise bottle fit.

Candle and fragrance packaging

Usually focuses on premium shelf presentation, scent variant systems, jar fit and matching box labels.

Wash care packaging

Usually needs stronger water resistance, scratch resistance and durable adhesive performance.

Small batch

Useful for new launches, market tests, limited product runs and first-time orders.

Bulk production

Roll labels support efficient application for repeat orders and packaging operations.

Materials & finishes

Common custom label options

Final material choice depends on bottle surface, label size, artwork color, finish requirement and order quantity.

Materials

Clear PET: A clean no-label look for transparent bottles and premium skincare packaging.

Waterproof BOPP: A practical option for cosmetic, shampoo, body wash and candle jar labels.

White vinyl: Durable and flexible for many bottle surfaces.

Textured paper: A tactile choice for selected premium packaging projects.

Finishes

Matte laminate: Soft, clean and professional surface feel.

Gloss laminate: Brighter color and stronger shine for shelf impact.

Foil stamping: Gold, silver or colored foil details for premium looks.

Die cut: Custom shapes for brand-specific packaging layouts.
