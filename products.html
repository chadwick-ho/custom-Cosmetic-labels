Materials & finishes

Common custom label options

Final material choice depends on bottle surface, label size, artwork color, finish requirement and order quantity.

Materials

Clear PET: A clean no-label look for transparent bottles and premium skincare packaging.

Waterproof BOPP: A practical option for cosmetic, shampoo and body wash labels.

White vinyl: Durable and flexible for many bottle surfaces.

Textured paper: A tactile choice for selected premium packaging projects.

Finishes

Matte laminate: Soft, clean and professional surface feel.

Gloss laminate: Brighter color and stronger shine for shelf impact.

Foil stamping: Gold, silver or colored foil details for premium looks.

Die cut: Custom shapes for brand-specific packaging layouts.
