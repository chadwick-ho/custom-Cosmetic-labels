Start with a purchasing question

Choose the decision you need to make, then use the detailed guide for the missing specification.

Choose material for the actual bottle

A material name does not establish adhesion, appearance or handling performance. Identify the bottle surface and relevant exposures before comparing constructions.

  • Compare material options
  • Review clear film and white ink
  • Prepare an adhesive sample plan

Release artwork with a clear reference

Review the actual output, its dimensions and assets. A digital proof checks layout; physical appearance and performance need the relevant sample review.

  • Separate trim, bleed and safe area
  • Check text in the PDF
  • Send linked image assets

Hand the labels to the filling site

Use the receiving operator's actual specification. Delivery format, label application and later use are separate questions.

  • Confirm roll and unwind requirements
  • Separate application and service temperatures
  • Plan unused-label storage

Place and repeat the right order

Specify quantity per design, the released revision and the actual in-hand requirement. A requested date becomes a commitment only when the relevant party confirms it.

  • Plan a small multi-SKU order
  • Lock a reorder reference
  • Plan lead-time milestones
