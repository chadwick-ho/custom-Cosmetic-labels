What can a purchasing guide establish?

A guide can explain a decision, identify missing specifications and provide an editorial checklist. It cannot establish that a particular adhesive works on your bottle, that an image is print-ready, or that a trading partner accepts your code. Those conclusions need the relevant project evidence and responsible approval.

Start with the container and quote checklist. General supply options belong to the cosmetic, wash care and candle and fragrance category pages.

How we distinguish sources from recommendations

Where a guide uses a manufacturer's technical document or a standards organisation's explanation, follow the linked source and its stated scope. A data sheet for one construction does not establish the specifications of another product. Editorial worksheets and conditional recommendations help organise a decision; they are not supplier test reports or a substitute for the applicable standard.

Examples described as hypothetical are planning scenarios, not customer interviews or completed orders. A source supporting a technical mechanism does not prove that a problem is common or that buyers have a particular budget.

What do the packaging images show?

Images captioned “AI-generated packaging example” are illustrative designs. Their brand layouts, bottles and planning cards are not evidence of a real customer order, factory process, certification or measured test result. Use the accompanying text to understand what the example does and does not demonstrate.

A packaging image or digital mockup cannot prove adhesion, exact printed colour, a functioning barcode or resistance to water and product contact. Ask for the actual sample and specification needed for the decision.

Order terms and approvals are project-specific

Confirm minimum quantity, quantity per design, material, finish, sampling, price, production and delivery in the written project proposal. A requested date is a planning input until confirmed. A digital proof concerns layout and copy; relevant physical properties need a representative sample review.
