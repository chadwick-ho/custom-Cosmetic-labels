FAQ

Candle and fragrance label FAQ

Can you make labels for candle jars and tins?

Yes. We support labels for glass candle jars, ceramic jars, metal tins, amber jars, clear jars and other home fragrance packaging.

Can you match labels with candle gift boxes?

Yes. We can prepare matching jar labels, lid labels, box labels and sealing labels for a consistent product collection.

Which finishes are common for candle labels?

Matte laminate, textured paper, clear PET, white BOPP and foil details are common choices for candle and fragrance packaging.

Can I order multiple scent names in one style?

Yes. We can support multiple scent variants with the same label structure, including small batch and bulk production.
