Should you choose clear film, opaque film or paper?

Clear film: consider it when the bottle and contents are part of the design. Review contrast and any white-ink layer on the actual filled container. Transparency alone will not make an adhesive edge disappear.

Opaque film: consider it when the artwork needs a consistent background across container colours. Match the adhesive and protective finish to actual use; a material name alone does not establish performance.

Paper: consider it for a tactile presentation on suitable dry packaging. If the pack will be handled wet or exposed to oils, compare a protected construction or film alternative using samples.

What are the MOQ, price and lead time?

Low-MOQ projects can be reviewed, but no single minimum or fixed price applies to every construction. Request a written quote showing quantity per design, material, finish, sampling, production and shipping. A requested deadline is a planning input until the supplier confirms it.

For example, 3 designs × 500 labels = 1,500 labels. That arithmetic describes your order; it is not a published minimum order or price tier.

What does a digital proof confirm?

A digital proof helps review text, layout, dimensions and artwork placement. It cannot by itself confirm adhesion, wet-use durability, exact physical colour or machine application. Use a physical sample on the filled container when those properties matter.
