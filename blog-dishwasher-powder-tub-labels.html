Dishwasher Powder Tub Labels: Work Around Lid and Dust

Published October 3, 2026 by YT Labels Editorial Team

A powder tub is not a gel bottle with a different product name. The wide screw lid changes where hands grip it, and powder dust changes how the pack looks on a worktable. I would choose the sidewall panel before asking for a larger plate graphic. The RINSELINE tubs shown here are generated packaging illustrations, not a real detergent or proof of cleaning performance.

The packaging photograph-style image below is AI-generated. Its invented brand and label artwork are illustrative, not a real customer product or factory test.

In this guide

  2. Give the back panel real space
  3. Evaluate the material with the filled pack

Keep the label below the screw lid

Measure the straight sidewall on the actual closed tub. Leave room beneath the lid skirt and above the bottom radius. A label too high can be scuffed each time the cap is removed; a label too low can wrinkle around the base. Check where the shopper or packing operator naturally grips the container.

The illustration uses a sidewall wrap and a printed roll. It is not a lid seal. If the project also needs a tamper feature or a top label, price and test that as a separate component with its own approved artwork.

Give the back panel real space

The front should make “Dishwasher Powder” easy to recognise. The responsible brand team must supply the actual directions, warnings, ingredients or other market-specific text for the back; a printer cannot infer them from the product category. The generated image intentionally does not supply usable dosing instructions.

Before reducing type to fit, check the planned label width on the tub. If approved copy is too dense, use a larger panel or a separate back label. I would not hide important information behind a decorative plate icon.

Evaluate the material with the filled pack

A powder pack may collect dust while a kitchen pack may meet damp hands. UPM Raflatac’s household-product label overview explains why chemical contact and use conditions matter to label selection. That does not promise this construction will work on every HDPE tub. Request a printed, applied sample and inspect the seam, rubbing and readability in your intended handling conditions.

If the label will be applied to a filled tub, ask when surface cleaning happens in the packing sequence. Adhesive performance cannot be judged from a photograph of a clean empty container.

Brief the full tub, not the old pouch

Send the tub dimensions and resin, lid style, panel measurements, approved copy, quantity by artwork, and roll-machine requirements if applicable. Specify whether there is a separate lid or bottom label. Ask for a digital proof and an applied sample before bulk print.
