Label Size Guide

How to Choose the Right Label Size for Bottles

A practical guide for cosmetic, skincare, shampoo, body wash and personal care bottle label projects.

Measure the available label area

Measure the flat or curved area where the label will sit. Include the label width, label height and any space needed around shoulders, grooves, seams or caps.

Send a bottle photo

A front photo and side photo help confirm whether the label should be rectangular, wraparound, front/back pair, round, oval or custom die cut.

Plan text before proofing

Front labels usually focus on brand name and product name. Back labels may carry ingredients, directions, barcode, batch area or other packaging information.

Confirm roll direction for production

If labels will be applied by machine or production line, share roll direction, gap, core size and application requirements before bulk production.
